Section 03

Official Logotype Architecture

Safety X-Height & Variants

Interactive Logo Canvas

Test logo legibility against different corporate backgrounds

Canvas:
1X Clearspace 1X Clearspace

Variant: Herbox Logo-red.png on Ivory

01. Clearspace Unit 'X'

Equal to the height of the capital letter 'H'. No type, borders, or iconography may penetrate this perimeter.

02. Print Minimum

Minimum width for physical offset or kraft print is 24 mm (0.95 in) to preserve bowl curves.

03. Digital Minimum

Minimum width for display / mobile screens is 88 pixels at 72 PPI (retina scaled 176px).

Strict Logo Violations (Don'ts)

Herbox

Do Not Distort / Stretch

Always scale proportionally with locked aspect ratio.

Herbox

No Drop Shadows or Bevels

Herbox is flat, crisp, and tactile. Never apply 3D skeuomorphism.

Herbox

No Low Contrast Combinations

Avoid dark green on sage or red on low-contrast backgrounds.

Herbox

Do Not Rotate or Slant

The logotype must strictly remain on horizontal baseline.
